# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Car and motorcycle owners across the Philippines often use the app on a phone, including on slow connections. They need to find a provider, book a visit, get roadside help, and follow work and payments.
- Service shop owners, independent mechanics, and shop staff need to see the work requiring their attention, record inspections and estimates, and complete repairs and billing.
- Administrators verify providers, review reports, and handle payment and settlement work.

## Product Purpose

Connect vehicle owners to verified service providers and keep each party informed from first contact through booking, repair, payment, and aftercare.

## Product Requirements

- Maintain nationwide shop and mobile-mechanic discovery and consistent Philippine local time in service flows.
- Make location sharing optional where possible. A roadside request needs an exact pickup point, visible to the customer and authorized responder.
- Keep the app usable in English, Bisaya, and Tagalog, on narrow screens, with a keyboard, and in both themes.
- Preserve the existing Laravel and Bootstrap app, its design tokens, and the admin sign-in verification flow.
